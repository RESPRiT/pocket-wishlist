History

[edit | edit source]
  • Mr. Accessory prices on all items increased by 1 during each new The Time-Twitching Tower event prior to 2023. For example during its initial release in the September 30, 2016 event, the KoL Con 13 snowglobe cost 1 Mr. A. During the October 11, 2022 event, they began costing 2 As, and so on.
    • An exception to the standard increase occurred with the introduction of level 9 on November 27, 2023 when only the KoL Con 13 snowglobe increased in price for this event.
    • And again on July 25th, 2024 when level 10 was released and only the wardrobe increased in price by 1.
